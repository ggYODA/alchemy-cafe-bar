About Alchemy Cafe Bar

Welcome to Alchemy Cafe Bar — a seaside café located on the beautiful shores of Nea Makri, Greece.

Whether you're looking for a morning coffee, a hearty lunch, evening drinks, or cocktails with a view of the Aegean Sea, Alchemy is your perfect destination.

Open from 09:00 to 00:00 every day, we welcome both locals and tourists seeking a relaxing atmosphere, fresh Mediterranean cuisine, and quality beverages.
